# Privacy Policy – google-health-claude

fitbit-claude is a personal project used only by its owner, Emil Shkurti.

- It reads the owner's own Google Health data (read-only) via the Google Health API.
- The data is stored in the owner's private GitHub repository and processed with Claude (Anthropic) for personal training and health analysis.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or collected from anyone else.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
